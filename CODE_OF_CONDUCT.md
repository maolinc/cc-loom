# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone.

Be respectful and constructive. Discuss ideas and code rather than attacking
people. Assume good intent, accept correction, protect private information,
and make space for people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention, and other
conduct that makes participation unsafe are not acceptable in project spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Maintainers
may edit or remove content, issue warnings, temporarily restrict participation,
or permanently ban participants when needed to protect the community. Reports
will be handled as confidentially as practical.
